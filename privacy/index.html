About this policy

This policy describes how the Taquin Élégance app for iPhone and iPad, published by BayDeck Team, handles your information.

Data collection

Taquin Élégance does not collect any data. We do not receive personal information, gameplay data or information about how you use the app.

Everything stays in the app

Your game progress, scores, settings and any photos used for puzzles stay locally within the app on your device. They are not sent to BayDeck Team or to a remote server.

Your photos

If you choose a photo to create a puzzle, it is used locally within the app for that purpose. Your photos are not collected, uploaded or shared by the app.

Sharing and tracking

Because the app does not collect data, we do not sell or share your information with third parties. We do not collect data for analytics, advertising or tracking.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
